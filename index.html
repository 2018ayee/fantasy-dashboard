How the playoff odds are modeled

The odds come from a player-level simulation built on this league's own box scores. Every rostered player's weekly points, weeks 1-11, feed a projection: an exponentially weighted average (the recency half-life and shrinkage are fit per position by walk-forward backtest on weeks 7-11, where the model beat a naive season average at every position) blended 65/35 with Sleeper's weekly projection, which carries matchup context. Each simulated week samples every player's score from a right-skewed Gamma whose variance is fit per position, zeroes players on bye (real NFL schedule) or out injured (live designations mapped to play probabilities), and scores each team with the actual best-ball operator: best QB, two RB, two WR, TE and two flex from that week's samples, so bench depth is priced by the scoring rule itself. Weeks 12-14 use the league's real pairings; the playoff is the league's real format (6 teams, top-2 byes, reseeded, weeks 15-17) with seeding ties broken on points-for carried through the simulation. The toilet bowl simulates both formats the league discussed for the bottom four. 5,000 season simulations. The full model code, backtest numbers and assumptions are attached to the "Simulated playoff odds" source, and FantasyCalc's own simulation is kept in the "Standings & playoff odds" source for comparison.

Values are FantasyCalc trade values built from millions of real fantasy trades, synced from this Sleeper league. Data files refresh on a weekly schedule; odds of exactly zero show as 0%, odds under 1% as <1%.

Live scores, game states and projections come straight from Sleeper’s public API while the page is open. Win odds, playoff sims and the toilet bowl come from the weekly model run · raw data.
